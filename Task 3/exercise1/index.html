Tribute to Albert Einstein

A famous scientist and thinker

About Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein was born in 1879 in the German Empire and died in 1955 in the United States. He became one of the most famous scientists in history. He is especially known for his work on relativity and his contributions to modern physics.

Life

His ideas changed the way people understand space, time, energy and gravity.

Important Events

  1. 1879 - Born in Ulm, Germany
  2. 1905 - Published several important scientific papers
  3. 1905 - Explained the photoelectric effect
  4. 1915 - Presented his general theory of relativity
  5. 1921 - Received the Nobel Prize in Physics
  6. 1955 - Died in Princeton, USA
